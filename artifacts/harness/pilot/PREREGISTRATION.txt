BACKGROUND
----------
Pilot study (n=15 episodes x 2 arms x 1 anatomy = VMR 0066) found:
  - Wire_only arm: low wrong-branch rate, few rotation steps
  - Overlay arm: elevated wrong-branch rate, excess rotation steps
Proposed mechanism: vessel overlay makes the rising-distance LM-bend illusion
visible, triggering excess rotation during LM traversal.

SCRIPTED-TRAVERSAL VERIFICATION (2026-07-01)
---------------------------------------------
Identical scripted controller (35mm/s advance + 1.0rad/s rotation), 3 seeds each:
  VMR 0066     : rise = +4.32mm mean (range 4.27-4.36mm), peak at step 6 in LM
                 15/15 seeds, illusion PRESENT. [From illusion_check.py]
  Normal_12    : rise = +0.00mm, peak at step 1, 0/3 seeds, illusion ABSENT
  Normal_5     : rise = +0.20mm in LM phase (steps 1-11), peak in LM, 0/3 seeds,
                 illusion ABSENT. (Post-LM rise is wrong-branch entry, not LM geometry.)

STUDY DESIGN — DISSOCIATION, NOT BLIND REPLICATION
----------------------------------------------------
The three anatomies are NOT equivalent replication instances.
They differ on a mechanistically critical variable: LM-bend illusion present/absent.

  Group A (illusion PRESENT) : VMR 0066
  Group B (illusion ABSENT)  : ASOCA Normal_12, ASOCA Normal_5

This is a DISSOCIATION design: the same overlay manipulation is applied across
both groups. If the mechanism is specific to the illusion, the effect should
track illusion status, not anatomy.

PRE-DECLARED PREDICTION (locked here before any VLM data on ASOCA anatomies)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PRIMARY:
  The overlay-arm excess rotation and elevated wrong-branch rate will appear in
  VMR 0066 (illusion present) and will be ATTENUATED OR ABSENT in Normal_12 and
  Normal_5 (illusion absent).

  Operationally: within-anatomy (wire_only vs overlay) OR(wrong_branch) difference
  will be positive and significant for VMR 0066, and near-zero or non-significant
  for Normal_12 and Normal_5.

ALTERNATIVE (disconfirmation):
  If overlay-arm excess rotation appears in ASOCA despite no LM-bend illusion,
  the mechanism is incomplete — bifurcation visibility alone drives rotation
  independent of the distance signal. This would change the paper's central claim.

ANALYSIS PLAN
-------------
Unit of analysis:   episode (single run from ostium insertion)
Primary outcome:    wrong_branch (binary: tip entered LCX at any step)
Secondary outcome:  excess_rotation_steps (count of rotate_cw/ccw actions per episode)

Per-anatomy analysis (main analysis):
  For each anatomy separately:
    Fisher's exact test: wrong_branch ~ arm (wire_only vs overlay)
  Report OR and 95% CI per anatomy.

Cross-anatomy dissociation analysis:
  The confirmatory pattern is:
    VMR 0066:   OR(overlay/wire_only) substantially > 1
    Normal_12:  OR(overlay/wire_only) near 1
    Normal_5:   OR(overlay/wire_only) near 1
  Formal test: Breslow-Day heterogeneity test on the 3-anatomy table
  (tests whether effect size is the same across anatomies; expect significant
  heterogeneity if the dissociation prediction is correct).

DO NOT:
  - Pool VMR 0066 with ASOCA anatomies into a single Mantel-Haenszel statistic
    for the wrong-branch outcome. They test different things.
  - Run post-hoc sub-analyses of ASOCA anatomies based on observed data before
    the cross-anatomy comparison is complete.
  - Tune system-prompt geometry parameters after seeing VLM outcomes
    (geometry params are fixed and derived from scripted-traversal diagnostics).

ACCEPTED POWER TRADE
---------------------
The dissociation framing sacrifices the pooled n≈75 power for wrong-branch
in exchange for mechanistic specificity. The wrong-branch PATTERN across
anatomies (present-with-illusion, absent-without) is the evidence, not a
single pooled significance test. This trade is deliberate and accepted.
