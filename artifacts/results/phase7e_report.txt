==========================================================================================
WHY BINARY OUTCOME AGREEMENT IS NOT REPORTED
==========================================================================================
  Every successful original episode crosses the 5 mm success threshold ON ITS FINAL
  RECORDED ACTION, arriving from a median 5.6 mm and clearing the line by roughly
  0.2 mm. The replay lands a median 0.3-0.9 mm from where the original finished. A
  margin of 0.2 mm against a divergence of 0.3 mm means binary agreement records
  which side of a knife edge the last step happened to fall on, not whether the
  replay navigated the same way. On A2, 25/25 replays finish within 5.5 mm while
  only 7/25 finish within 5.0 mm - they are terminating a fraction short, not
  failing to navigate.

  The metric is therefore WITHDRAWN rather than repaired, and replaced by the
  continuous quantity reported above: replay-vs-original final-distance divergence.
  Lengthening the replay to chase the threshold would manufacture agreement without
  making the comparison more faithful.
