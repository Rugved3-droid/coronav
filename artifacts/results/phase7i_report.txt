========================================================================================
DECISION RULE, STATED BEFORE THE NUMBERS WERE SEEN
========================================================================================
  correlation near zero  -> seed-matching removes no shared variance; pairing is
                            unjustified; the unpaired Mann-Whitney is primary.
  correlation positive   -> pairing does real work; the paired result stays primary.
  Both tests are reported in the paper either way, with the correlation given as the
  justification for the choice.
