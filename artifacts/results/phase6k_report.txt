====================================================================================
ONE-PARAGRAPH SUMMARY
====================================================================================
  Both A3 arms enter the 33-38 mm band at a similar point along the traversed
  path: median arc length 34.3 mm (wire-only) vs 35.0 mm (overlay),
  i.e. 50% vs 76% of the path each episode ultimately travels.
  Local path curvature at that locus is 0.509 1/mm (wire-only) and
  0.697 1/mm (overlay), against a whole-path median of 0.365 1/mm,
  so the band is not a locally sharp bend relative to the rest of the course.
  What separates the arms is dwell, not location: a median of 60 steps
  inside the band for wire-only against 31 for overlay. The stall is
  therefore better described as time spent in a region both arms reach than as a
  geometric obstacle only one arm encounters. Because A3's wall is a generated
  constant-radius tube, no calibre narrowing exists there by construction.
