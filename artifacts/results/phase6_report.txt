DID EXCLUDING A3 EASE THE MULTIPLICITY CORRECTION?
  The question matters because A3 supplied the largest effect available to us, so its
  removal has to be shown not to have been self-serving. Below, each declared member's
  adjusted p under the family as declared, against what it WOULD have been had the
  excluded geometry been counted. A3's own adjusted p is not computed for reporting
  and appears nowhere: it is not in a family.

  anatomy         raw p   Holm (declared, 2)   Holm (if 3)    Bonf (2)   Bonf (if 3)
  A1             0.0147               0.0147        0.0147      0.0294        0.0442
  A2           0.000482             0.000965      0.000965    0.000965       0.00145

  NO. Every declared member's Holm-adjusted p is IDENTICAL under either family
  size. Holm leaves the largest p in a family unmultiplied, so A1 is unadjusted
  either way; A2 is the middle of three and the smaller of two, taking the same
  multiplier in both. The exclusion bought us nothing on the primary adjustment.
  Only Bonferroni is sensitive to the family size, and Bonferroni is not the
  primary adjustment.
