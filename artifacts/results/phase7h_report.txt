========================================================================================
PHASE 7h — IS THE VARIABILITY EXCESS, OR BINOMIAL?
========================================================================================

H0 under test: the seed carries no information and each episode is an independent
Bernoulli draw. If the data are consistent with that, the correct Methods statement
is 'the seed does not propagate to outcomes', NOT 'the simulator is unreliable'.

========================================================================================
A1 VMR0066   R=5 repetitions x n=25 seeds
========================================================================================
  success counts per repetition: [16, 15, 15, 11, 12]
  pooled success rate p = 69/125 = 0.5520

  1. CONCORDANCE ACROSS ALL REPETITIONS
     P(same outcome in all 5 | independence) = p^5 + (1-p)^5 = 0.0693
     expected concordant seeds = 1.73 of 25
     observed concordant seeds = 4 of 25
     exact binomial test vs independence: p = 0.091
     -> MORE concordant than independence predicts

  2. DISPERSION OF THE SUCCESS COUNT
     observed variance across repetitions = 4.700
     binomial variance n*p*(1-p)          = 6.182
     variance ratio                       = 0.760
     dispersion statistic X2 = 3.041 on 4 df
       P(X2 >= observed) = 0.551   (over-dispersion)
       P(X2 <= observed) = 0.449   (under-dispersion)
     -> CONSISTENT WITH BINOMIAL: no evidence of excess variability

  3. STEPS-TO-TARGET VARIANCE COMPONENTS
     successful episodes: 69   distinct step counts: [27]
     mean WITHIN-seed variance across repetitions = 0.0000
     BETWEEN-seed variance of per-seed means      = 0.0000
     -> DEGENERATE: every successful episode takes an identical number of
        steps, so the continuous endpoint has ZERO variance both within and
        across seeds. There is no steps variability to decompose; under a
        fixed policy ALL variability lives in the binary outcome.

========================================================================================
WHAT THIS MEANS FOR METHODS
========================================================================================
  If both anatomies are consistent with binomial, the honest statement is that the
  simulator seed does not propagate to episode outcomes - episodes behave as
  independent draws - and NOT that the environment is unreliable. The run-to-run
  range measured in 7g is then simply sampling variability at n=25, which is what
  any n=25 binary endpoint carries, and is not evidence of a defect.
