====================================================================================================
PHASE 6e — API FAILURE AUDIT (selection-bias test, not bookkeeping)
====================================================================================================

A failed call is NOT skipped: run_coronary.py executes [[0.0, 0.0]] — a null HOLD —
and continues. Every failure inside a retained episode therefore consumes one step
of that episode's budget. That is the bias-relevant quantity.

Whole-run outages (every call failed) are removed by the Phase 6d exclusion rule and
cannot bias the analysed data; they dominate the RAW per-call counts, so raw rates
are reported but are NOT the basis for the arm comparison.
