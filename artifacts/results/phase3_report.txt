STATUS OF THE SUPERSEDED ANALYSIS
  The manuscript's steps-to-target comparison was restricted to successful
  episodes. Success is an outcome of the condition, and the arms contribute
  unequal subsets (20 vs 13), so that comparison is INVALID and is REPLACED
  by the three analyses below. It is not re-weighted, and no claim is made
  that the replacement is 'stronger' or 'weaker' than it: the two estimate
  different quantities on different samples under different designs, and are
  therefore not placed on a common scale anywhere in this report.

4. RECONCILIATION: log-rank vs Fisher on the SAME 50 episodes
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Fisher exact on terminal success (20/25 vs 13/25): two-sided p = 0.0718, OR = 3.692
  Log-rank on step-to-target with censoring: p = 0.000623

  Both are correct, and they do not conflict: Fisher uses ONLY the terminal
  success proportion at the budget, discarding when each episode succeeded,
  whereas the log-rank uses the step at which every episode either reached
  target or was censored — so it detects the overlay arm's systematically
  later successes that Fisher is blind to.

  The manuscript's success-rate claim REMAINS DECLINED (Fisher p = 0.0718, n.s.). The log-rank is a statement about TIME TO TARGET,
  not about the terminal proportion, and must not be reported as reviving
  the abandoned success-rate claim.
